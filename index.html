About the property

The sellers are motivated. This one must be sold.

The sellers are highly motivated and have made it clear — this property needs to be sold by auction day. Prior offers are encouraged. If you have been looking at Yarrabilba, stop looking and start acting.

Some homes look good before you even walk through the door. The clean modern front, the timber entry and the neat, well-kept street appeal tell you straight away how this home has been looked after. Step inside and the scale of the place hits you. This is not your standard Yarrabilba build. The rooms are generous, the proportions are right and there is room to breathe in every direction.

The open-plan kitchen, living and dining room is the social heart of the home and it is genuinely large — mocha cabinetry, stone benchtops, a stainless rangehood and a breakfast bar that pulls people in. A separate lounge and media room gives the household a proper second space to retreat to at the end of the day.

Outside, the alfresco at over five and a half metres wide is fan-cooled, fully tiled and flows straight to a generous lawn wrapped in tropical gardens. Once you are out here, you will not want to come back inside.
